**Chosen: A**, because the line is owned (the prompt line → the film timeline → the hyphen in the repo's own name) and it is buildable in HTML/GSAP. **Grafted from B:** the real prompt text is typed in the product's own input during the breath.

**Engine lock (Move 4b)**: primary engine `motionmaxxing` · engine-ban check: PASS vs motionmaxxing Never-ship 1-12 (no HUD/counters/tracked caps; skill names are lowercase content in the world, not labels; typing only in the product's own prompt input; no colour-flood wipe: the ground change is a light event at the drop, lit by a key, not a wipe; no web-footer end card: mark only, hold ≤ 1.4 s; no stat slide; the line→hyphen is the film's device, not a dot-to-logo). Conflicts resolved for motionmaxxing: words 2-4 f apart in the hook, Inter only, generated (mgaudio CC0) music.

## 1. Director's Treatment
- **PAGE**: Problem: too many ways in (59 skills, nine engines, gate words) · Audience: motion designers / agent users on a launch post, sound on · Goal: repeat "one prompt in, one directed film out" · Emotion: overwhelmed → in control
- **The one true thing**: `video-director` locks ONE engine from many and judges the render before it ships.
- **Idea** (≤ 8 words): One prompt turns the noise into one line.
- **Device**: the line → path: prompt field underline (breath) → the line every name collapses into (hero) → the film's timeline (echo) → the hyphen in "Motion-DESIGNER" (mark).
- **Grammar**: before Enter everything moves diagonally and nothing lines up; after Enter everything moves only along one horizontal line and the film gets quieter.
- **Logline** (seen): a flood of tool names freezes, one prompt is typed, Enter pulls them all into one line.
- **Idea sentence**: In this film, **the repo's own pile of skill names** becomes **one straight line**.
- **Hero moment** (the screenshot frame): 12.0-14.5 s (centre 66 %). Enter at 12.00 → 2-frame light-out (frames 360-361) → key light up, warm ground → ~38 names on 3 depths travel in ONE continuous 20-frame move (frames 362-382, whip ease, x-smear 40 px decaying over 3 f, scaleY 1 → 0.08) into a single 3 px line at y 540 spanning x 160-1760; the poster holds 1.2 s (frames 386-421) with `motionmaxxing` lifted 46 px above the line in accent and the prompt riding the line's left end; everything inside an 80 px margin.
- **System decisions**: Clock smooth 30 · Motion grammar directional smear on every fast move (PR-A23) · Type Inter 400-550, sentence case; copy 7-9 % H, names 2.2-6 % H, mark 11 % H · Accent #FF4F1A = "the director's choice" (locked engine, PASS, the hyphen; one element per scene) · Carrier the line · Cursor n/a (keyboard) · Overshoot spent on none · Scale spent on the 9 engine names arriving from 3.5x (build) and the hero collapse
- **World per act**:
  | Act | Ground | Light (+ light event) | Depth | Texture | Sound |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1 Overwhelm 0-11 s | warm black #0E0D0C, deep space | cold sweeps of light crossing the name wall, faster each bar (event: sweeps strobe at 10.5) | 3 parallax depths 1.0 / 0.6 / 0.3, far layer blurred 6 px | none on purpose, motion blur only | techno build, hats on 8ths, whooshes on whips |
  | 2 Breath 11-12 s | near-black | one key pool on the prompt field (event: everything dims to one pool) | the frozen wall ghosted at 8 % far behind | none | near-silence: room tone + irregular keystrokes |
  | 3 Control 12-20 s | warm paper #ECE6DA lit gradient | 2-frame light-out then a warm key (largest luma event of the film); end: pool rises 6 % over the hold | the line sharp, thumbnails 0.6 depth | soft paper vignette | drop at 12.0, groove, subtraction into the mark |
- **Energy curve**: 2.5 s → 2.0 s → 1.5 s → 1.0 s → 1.0 s → 0.5 s → 0.25 s stutter → breath held 1.0 s → hero 2.5 s (longest shot after the drop) → 0.75 s → 0.75 s → 4.0 s resolve
- **Sound arc**: world sound keystrokes + whooshes → first hit at 6.0 s (engines arrive) → quiet keystrokes 11.0-11.45 s then true silence 11.5-12.0 s (music muted, sends cut: the breath) → drop at 12.0 s on the hero (Enter click + impact + sub) → groove under the echoes → subtraction from 16.0 s into the mark hit at 18.75 s → tail decays to digital silence by 20.0 s
- **Ending**: the mark arrives by the line contracting (it pulls "Motion" and "DESIGNER" in from its ends), built from the line itself (the hyphen is the line); hold 1.15 s (18.85-20.0 s) with a 1.00 → 1.02 scale breathe and the light pool rising; final frame = poster
- **Banned for this film**: play-button icon, film reel, clapperboard, glowing AI orb, rocket; + Part B tells (no crossfades, no flash transitions, no centred title page on a flat field)

## 2. Format & Specs
- `1920x1080` (`16:9`), `30 fps`, `20.0 s` (600 f), deliverable MP4 (H.264 + AAC)
- Palette: #0E0D0C ground act 1-2 (55 %), #ECE6DA ground act 3 (40 %), ink #EDE9E1 / #141210, accent #FF4F1A (≤ 2 %) · Typography: Inter, 80 px safe margin
- Audio: mgaudio `techno` recipe (CC0 VCSL samples + synthesis), 120 BPM, -14 LUFS, ≤ -1.1 dBTP (master -2.5 dBTP for AAC)
- Constraints: no HUD/chrome, names are the real `.agents/skills/` folders and real script names

## 3. Exact Ordered Skill Chain
