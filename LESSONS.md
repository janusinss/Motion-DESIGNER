# LESSONS

What independent juries said this repo's films get wrong, as rules the next film follows. `video-director` reads the last 10 entries at step 2 and appends one at delivery (step 8). Append only; never edit an earlier entry.

Entry format: `## YYYY-MM-DD · <slug> · <engine> · jury <total>/10 (<lens>)`, then lowest dimensions, then rules.

---

## 2026-11-27 · motion-designer-hype · motionmaxxing · jury 7.05/10 (Motion Awards)
Lowest: World/Light 6, Originality 6, Idea 7, Motion Craft 7, Design 7 (r1 6.65 → r2 6.70 → r3 7.05; capped at 3 rounds).
- **Peak at the hero**: the drop needs the largest luma event of the film (a 2-frame light-out, then the key light) or the measured energy peak lands in the build instead (r1 peaked at 49 %).
- **Hero = one readable move**: a 0.5 s collapse reads as a hit, not as "order arriving". Make it one continuous move of ~14-22 f with a ≥0.5 s held poster frame inside an 80 px margin.
- **No clipped text**: fade long lists into the ground; size terminal lines so the longest one keeps an 80 px right margin at the end of its push.
- **Build the end mark from the film's material**: a stock wordmark on a flat ground scored "generic" in every round; grain alone does not keep a hold alive.
- **Chaos must be choreographed**: cap ~7 sharp elements at once, 3 parallax depths; random overlaps read as mush, not as "too many".
- **Proof beats show transformation, not readouts**: two terminal panels in a row read as a slide deck.
- **Seek-safety**: two keyframes on the same frame are not seek-safe (selfTest mismatch); hold for one frame instead.
- **AAC headroom**: master to -2.5 dBTP before an AAC mux; AAC adds ~1.2 dB of true peak on hard transients.
